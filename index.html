Help & support

Controls, checkpoints and practical help for DOG & TOAST.

Updated 2026-10-24 · Crumb Lantern

Free levels and the full game

The App Store version is free to download and levels 1–10 are free to play. One purchase unlocks the full 50-level campaign, including levels 11–50. No subscription or automatic renewal. The US standard price is US$4.99; your App Store confirmation shows the applicable local price.

Restore purchases and keep your progress

Open Full game → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that bought it. This restores access, not deleted local saves. Buying or restoring does not reset levels, checkpoints, bones or best results, and does not complete levels for you.

Purchase problems and refunds

Cancellation, a failed payment or a pending approval does not unlock paid levels or erase progress. Check your connection and Apple Account, then retry or restore. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com; the studio cannot issue App Store refunds. Do not email card details or passwords.

Touch controls

Use the left control to move. On the right, Jump jumps, Toast throws or recalls toast, and Woof activates bark-responsive mechanisms. Bark uses a button, not your microphone.

Keyboard and gamepad

Keyboard: A/D or left/right arrows move; Space/W/up arrow jumps; J/Left Shift throws or recalls; B barks; Esc/P pauses. Gamepad: left stick, bottom/left/top face buttons, and Start/Menu respectively.

Using toast

Press the toast action to throw it; press again to recall it. Depending on the mechanism, toast can become a foothold, hold a plate or power a receiver. It is not a weapon that defeats every creature.

Continue, checkpoints and restart

Continue loads the last saved level and checkpoint progress. Before activating a flag, death returns you to the level start; afterwards it returns you to that flag. Restart begins this level again and keeps best records.

Adjusting buttons

In Settings you can change the touch-button size and open Adjust controls to drag the controls. Save applies the layout; Back cancels the layout preview. Language changes do not erase progress.

If sound is missing

Check Music and Sound in Settings, device volume and the current speaker/headphone route. Return from the background or reopen the game. Avoid uninstalling just to troubleshoot sound: local progress may be lost.

If you are stuck or see stutter

Pause and retry from a safe position. Restart resets the current level, including its current flag. If performance drops, let the device cool and report the level and location; no fixed device frame rate is promised.

Report a problem

Email jianchen120695@gmail.com with the level, what happened, steps to repeat it, device/OS and game version. Screenshots or logs are optional. Do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive details.
