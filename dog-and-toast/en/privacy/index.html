Privacy policy

How DOG & TOAST handles local progress, support messages and these pages.

Updated 2026-10-24 · Crumb Lantern

Who makes the game

Crumb Lantern makes DOG & TOAST. The developer credited in the game is jianchenliu. This notice covers the current game, these help pages and voluntary support requests.

Progress stays on your device

The game saves your current level, checkpoint, collected bones, puzzle progress, best results, language, audio, vibration and control layout locally. No player account is required.

No advertising or tracking service

The game has no integrated advertising or analytics SDK. Game code does not upload progress, build an advertising profile or provide custom cloud sync.

Permissions and the bark button

Game code does not request your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library. Bark is a game action and plays a sound; it does not record your voice.

The engine, Apple and device backups

The game uses Unity. Apple handles store downloads, purchases and optional system diagnostics under its own policies. OS backups may include local app data; the game does not guarantee cross-device recovery.

Apple purchase information

Apple processes the optional one-time full-game purchase. StoreKit supplies verified purchase status and local store pricing; the studio does not receive your card details or App Store password. Restoring a purchase restores access, not local level saves.

Local development diagnostics

Development/test builds can write local device, OS, performance and game-event logs. These are not automatically sent to the studio. There is no automatic support attachment or crash-upload service in the current game.

When you open a website

These public pages use GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub login, under its privacy statement. Our pages add no advertising, analytics, cookies or browser storage. Opening email uses your mail provider; no saves or logs are attached automatically.

Messages you choose to send

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use these to investigate and reply. The support mailbox uses Gmail (Google); Gmail and your email provider process messages under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. Email us about access or deletion. Removing the app may remove local progress; separately manage any OS backups. Local settings are not encrypted storage for sensitive information.

Younger players

Gameplay does not ask for a name, age or contact details. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information. If a child has sent personal information, a parent or guardian can email us to request deletion.

Changes and contact

We update this notice when the relevant game or support practices change. The date above identifies this version. For privacy questions or requests, email jianchen120695@gmail.com.
